# Logo options

Three candidates for the plugin icon, all about watching together and chatting (Kino palette: red #E50914, dark
#0D0D0D, white). Each has its SVG source and PNG renders at 512, 192 and 48 px. Variant A is the provisional
`icon.svg` / `icon.png` until the owner picks one; this folder goes away after that choice.

- **A** `a-screen-chat`: a screen with a play button, and two chat bubbles (one typing, one with text).
- **B** `b-shared-bubbles`: two overlapping chat bubbles, two people, sharing one play triangle.
- **C** `c-popcorn-chat`: a popcorn bucket with a play triangle, and a chat bubble with a tick.
